Scanny — Free Offline Document Scanner

Scanny is a free privacy-first document scanner that runs entirely on your device. Scan with your camera, auto-detect edges, apply filters, run offline OCR, export to PDF, Word, CSV, JPG or PNG. Transfer between devices with one QR code or fully offline via camera. No account. No server. No tracking.
